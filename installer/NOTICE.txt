What this installer does on your computer:
 - copies the engine (rhcsd.exe), the PULSE website files and a few small
   scripts into the folder you choose (default: your user profile, no
   administrator rights needed);
 - creates a private access token and a settings file (.env) in that folder;
   the engine listens on this computer only (127.0.0.1:7878);
 - registers the pulse: link (so a web page can ask your computer to START
   the engine, and nothing else), and, if you leave the option ticked, starts
   the engine when you sign in. Both are for your Windows user only.

Uninstalling (Settings > Apps > PULSE) stops the engine and removes all of it.
